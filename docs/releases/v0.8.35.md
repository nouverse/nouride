**0.8.35 updates from 0.8.0 or later.** From 0.7.x, start 0.8.0 once first:
[Upgrading to 0.8](https://nouride.com/en/docs/deployment/upgrading-to-0-8/) has the way across.

On Docker, check your configuration with the new image before you switch:
`docker run --rm -v ./nouride:/app/.nouride ghcr.io/nouverse/nouride:0.8.35 nouride config check`, with the volume your
compose file mounts.

### Automations and missions are each person's own

- **Each person's automations and missions are their own.** Every account used to see and change every automation and
  every mission. Now each one belongs to a person: an automation to whoever made it on the dashboard or turned it on
  after an agent proposed it, a mission to whoever started it, from a chat or from the Missions page. A mission an
  automation starts belongs to that automation's owner. The Automations and Missions pages show your own.
- **What reports to your chat, you can read.** An automation or a mission of someone else's that reports to a chat of
  yours is on your page too, saying whose it is. You open it and read its steps and its runs, and you cannot change it.
- **An operator or a viewer sees no one else's**, not in the lists and not by its link: Nouride answers as if it were
  not there. The conversation an automation or a mission keeps of its own is held the same way, where every account
  could read it before.
- **An admin sees everyone's**, and picks whose in a dropdown beside the search, as on Chats: their own, everyone's set
  apart by person, one person's, or the ones that are nobody's. While the page shows an admin's own, it says how many
  more there are. On an automation's page an admin hands it to a person, or to nobody.
- **Only its owner or an admin changes one**: edits, runs, pauses or deletes an automation, answers a step that waits,
  and tells, stops, continues, accepts or deletes a mission. One an agent proposed is the exception: whoever may open
  the chat it was proposed in says yes, and it becomes theirs. A change an agent proposes to an automation that is
  already there is its owner's or an admin's to say yes to.
- **A webhook's address is shown only to whoever may change it.**
- This is about the pages. An agent still answers from every automation and mission it has, whoever made them: two
  people who must not learn of each other's work want two agents.

### Before you upgrade

Nothing to do first. What is different after it:

- **The Automations and Missions pages open on your own.** What belongs to someone else, or to nobody, is under
  **Everyone** in the dropdown beside the search, for an admin. Nothing is deleted or paused: every automation runs as
  it did.
- **Who owns what from before** is worked out once, when Nouride first starts on 0.8.35. An automation belongs to the
  account that made it or turned it on, or to the person who turned it on in a chat. A mission belongs to whoever
  started it. One made with the `nouride` command, or by an account that is gone, is nobody's: an admin finds it under
  **Nobody's** and hands it to a person on its page. Nothing is given to anyone by a name that only looks like theirs.
- **An automation turned on in a chat app is yours on the dashboard once People knows that account is you.** If your
  Telegram, Discord or WhatsApp account is not under your name in People yet, add it there, and what you turned on from
  that chat shows as yours.
- **On an install with several accounts, an operator no longer changes another person's automation or mission**, and
  no longer sees it unless it reports to a chat of theirs. Where an operator looks after automations someone else made,
  an admin hands each one to them on its page.
- The `nouride` command sees and changes every one, as before.
