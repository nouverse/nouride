### Chat

- **An agent says what it is about to do before long work.** When a request takes several steps, the agent's first sentence ("I'll check the 52 documents one by one") reaches the chat at once, before the work, and a short status follows on long work, at most every two minutes. On every platform, WhatsApp included, which had no sign of life during a long turn. A question answered at once is one message, as before. `[llm.streaming] progress_in_chat = false` turns it off with the progress line.
- **An agent checks a change before it calls it done.** A file written or copied, a cell set, a service restarted, a message sent elsewhere: it reads it back first, or says it could not.

### Groups

- **Rooms have names.** A group, channel or thread is called what its platform calls it: a WhatsApp group's subject, a Telegram group's title, a Discord `#channel` or a thread's name. `send_message` finds a group by that name.
- **A Groups page beside People.** Every room your agents talk in, with its platform, its agents and who talks there, by their People names. Rename a room there; that name wins over the platform's, for the agents too.
- **A Discord thread starts with the message it was opened from**, so an agent tagged in a new thread knows what it is about.

### Documents

- **Reading any document is one command.** The bundled *document reading* skill now ships `doc.py`: `inspect` tells what a file really is and what is in it (a workbook's real used range, headers, hidden sheets, merged cells, formulas; a document's outline and tracked changes; a PDF's text layer or a scan; encrypted or damaged files refused plainly), `extract` writes a sheet or a document to a file, and `query` answers SQL over a sheet or a CSV without loading it into the conversation.
- **Editing a workbook keeps what Excel needs.** The *document authoring* skill ships `xlsx_edit.py`: it edits a copy, refuses what would silently drop charts, macros or validation, and reads the copy back before it reports.

### Dashboard

- A long link in your own message wraps inside it, and the Reasoning toggle's focus ring is the size of the toggle.
