**0.8.41 updates from 0.8.0 or later.** From 0.7.x, start 0.8.0 once first:
[Upgrading to 0.8](https://nouride.com/en/docs/deployment/upgrading-to-0-8/) has the way across.

On Docker, check your configuration with the new image before you switch:
`docker run --rm -v ./nouride:/app/.nouride ghcr.io/nouverse/nouride:0.8.41 nouride config check`, with the volume your
compose file mounts.

A release about keeping hold of things. A mission can be paused and gone on with, and one held up for its model says
when it tries again. An agent can be deleted. A whole install can be taken to another machine. And a list of people
can be let in ahead, on WhatsApp, Telegram and Discord.

### A mission can be paused

A mission at work could only be stopped for good.

- **Pause holds a mission where it is.** **Pause** on its page or card, `/mission pause <id>` in its chat, or
  `nouride mission pause <id>`. Its turn at work is cut, its background jobs are ended, and its folder and its work
  are kept. A mission waiting in the queue can be paused too.
- **It stays paused as long as you like.** The time counts against none of its limits and nobody is reminded of it.
  **Resume**, or `/mission continue <id>`, lets it go on from its checklist.
- **A paused mission is under Needs you with a Paused tag**, and says who paused it.
- **The orchestrator can pause any agent's mission when you ask it to.** It can go on only with one it paused itself:
  a mission you paused is yours to resume.

### A mission held up for its model says when it tries again

A mission whose model refused, at a plan's limit for example, sat under Needs you as "paused, retrying". It did try
again by itself, and nothing on the page said so or when.

- **It is "Held up", in the Working column**, and its card and page say "It tries again by itself in 43 min".
- **The chat's notice names the rhythm**: after 2, 10 and 30 minutes, then every hour.
- **A turn that got something done before it was refused starts the count again.** The mission is held up anew, you
  are told again, and its next try is 2 minutes off. Its next try could be an hour off before, with nobody told.
- **"Goes on" is said once the mission is at work again**, not hours later when a long turn ends.
- `nouride mission list` and `/mission` in a chat say "held up, tries again by itself".

### A mission that asked a question waits for the answer

A mission that asked something, went on with the rest and finished it was offered for acceptance as if it were done,
with its question still open.

- **It waits for your answer instead**, and its chat is told once that everything else is finished.
- **Your answer gives it a turn**, since the answer may change the work, and it says it is done after that.
- Accepting it as it stands still closes it.

### Fewer messages about a draft

One draft could send four messages to a chat: the draft, each change to it, and a reminder four hours later.

- **Nobody is reminded of a draft.** It waits in Backlog until you start it. A result waiting to be accepted is still
  followed up, once.
- **A change to a draft rewrites its notice where it stands**, with its buttons, on Telegram and Discord. Where a
  notice cannot be rewritten, one line says what changed.

### An agent can be deleted

- **An admin deletes an agent at the foot of its Settings tab**, or with `nouride agent delete <id>`.
- **What makes it run goes**: its pack, its private folder with its coding CLI's sign-ins, its entries in the
  configuration, its automations, the connections only it answers, its seat in rooms, its open missions, and its own
  system user where agents have one.
- **What happened stays**: its chats, what it remembered, the records of its finished missions, the history and the
  usage. An agent made later with the same id is joined to them again.
- **The confirmation is in three steps**: a dialog that lists what goes, with counts and the folder's size, then the
  agent's id typed, then ten seconds before the button can be pressed.
- **The orchestrator cannot be deleted.** Make another agent the orchestrator first. No agent can delete an agent.

### An install taken to another machine

- **Settings has a page for it, Export and import**, for an admin, with `nouride export --out <file>` and
  `nouride import <file>` beside it.
- **Export writes one file you can open with ordinary tools**: the database, the configuration, the people, the
  agents' packs, the memory, the skills, the documents and attachments. The secrets are in it sealed to a passphrase
  you choose then. The agents' private folders are in it when you tick them, their size shown first.
- **Import replaces what the install holds**, after a snapshot of it and the same three-step confirmation, and then
  Nouride restarts. A new install's setup page offers it too.
- **After an import nothing goes out by itself.** The connections are off and the automations held until you press
  **Go live**, which turns on what was on.
- A file from an older version is brought forward as an update would. One from a newer version is refused with
  "Update Nouride first". A wrong passphrase is refused before anything is replaced.
- Not in the file: a coding CLI's sign-ins, which you make again on the new machine, the snapshots, and installed
  tools.
- The guide: [Move Nouride to another machine](https://nouride.com/en/docs/deployment/move-to-another-machine/).

### A whole list of people let in ahead

Somebody answering a contact list of thousands could let each person in one command at a time. Asked for in
issue #27.

- **`nouride access allow --from <file> --gateway <connection>`** lets in a whole file, one person a line, with a
  name after a comma if you have one. `--dry-run` counts and writes nothing. A spreadsheet saved as CSV works as it is.
- **WhatsApp takes phone numbers with the country code**, written however you like. **Telegram and Discord take an
  account's id or its username.**
- **The command says how it went**: how many are new, in already, blocked and kept out, said twice, and which lines
  it could not read. Up to 50,000 lines a run, and one line in History for the list.
- **A list of WhatsApp numbers lets those people in.** WhatsApp delivers most people under a hidden id, not their
  number, so a number let in ahead matched nobody. Nouride now knows the number behind the hidden id and lets a
  person in by either. A block holds by either too.
- **Somebody let in by username is known by that username.** If they give it up and somebody else takes it, that
  somebody is let in. Use ids where that matters.
- **The Approved tab lists the newest 500 and says how many there are.** Type an id or a name and press **Search
  everyone** for one beyond them, or `nouride access approved --find <id or name>`.
- WhatsApp here is not the official Business API: it links a number as WhatsApp Web does, and WhatsApp can restrict
  or ban a number that sends in bulk to people who never wrote to it.
- The manual: [Access](https://nouride.com/en/docs/manual/access/).

### The dashboard inside Nouride Home

- **No Sign out for the session a Home app made for itself.** A browser on the same phone keeps its Sign out.
- **The way back to Nouride Home is a labelled button inside the page**, on the sign-in, welcome and setup pages. It
  was a bare arrow floating over them.
- **Back and "Back to Nouride Home" are two things**, in a Home app that knows both: Back pages back, and "Back to
  Nouride Home" asks at once, whatever the page. This is Nouride's half. The Home apps use it from a release of
  their own, and in the apps installed today both go back a page, as before.

### Fixes

- **A coding CLI's model field lists every model when you press it**, as the chat's model picker does. It listed
  nothing until something was typed. On a phone the list still waits for typing or the arrow, so it does not open
  under the keyboard.
- **A mission whose helpers are still at work is not called stuck.** An agent that looked in on jobs still running
  three times had its mission held as stuck, with the work going on behind it.
