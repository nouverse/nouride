**0.8.43 updates from 0.8.0 or later.** From 0.7.x, start 0.8.0 once first:
[Upgrading to 0.8](https://nouride.com/en/docs/deployment/upgrading-to-0-8/) has the way across.

On Docker, check your configuration with the new image before you switch:
`docker run --rm -v ./nouride:/app/.nouride ghcr.io/nouverse/nouride:0.8.43 nouride config check`, with the volume your
compose file mounts.

A release that finishes what 0.8.42 began: a skill sooner for work done in one chat, what a coding CLI's commands
left running ended with their mission, and a connection that says what it waits on.

### A skill at the third sighting

0.8.42 wrote a new skill once the work came back in the same chat after six hours away. For somebody who works with
their agent in one chat all day, that was still too long a wait: a kind of work seen five times in a day stayed
"seen, not a skill yet", because the widest gap between two sittings was five hours forty.

- **The third time a new kind of work is seen, its skill is written**, whatever the time between. Work that comes
  back in another conversation, or in the same chat after six hours away, is still written at its second sighting.
- **Work seen once is still only noted.** A one-off does not become a skill.
- **The Skills page says when.** Under **Pending**, a kind of work seen twice or more reads "It becomes a skill the
  next time this work is seen."
- **"What learning did lately" says the same thing once.** A kind of work held seven times in an afternoon was
  seven of its twelve lines. It is one line now, "(7 times)", with the newest shown.

### What a coding CLI left running ends with its mission

0.8.42 ended what a mission's commands left running, for the commands Nouride runs. An agent on a coding CLI
runs its commands from the CLI's own shell, and what one of those started and did not wait for could go on after
the mission, with nothing to say whose it was.

- **What a coding CLI's commands started and left is ended when the mission ends**, in the same notice as the rest.
- **What set itself apart is left, and named.** A build's own daemon, which stays up for the next build, keeps
  running, and the mission's chat is told it is still there: "2 processes it started are still running, set apart in
  a session of their own, and were left (java (2))."
- This needs an install whose service hands its control group over, which a systemd install does. In a container a
  coding CLI's commands are not followed, and what they leave is left, as before. Claude Code, which serves each
  conversation from a process of its own, is followed; a CLI that serves them all from one process is not.

### A connection says what it waits on

A chat app that asks a bot to come back later is waited out by its client library without a word. A Telegram
connection once read "connecting" for half an hour, after several restarts close together, with nothing in the log
and nothing on its row to tell a wait from a connection that had hung.

- **The reason is on the connection's card**, with when it connects where the app named a time: "Connects in 28
  min. Telegram asked this bot to wait 30 minutes before it connects…" It is also in `nouride status`, among
  `/health`'s issues, and once in the log.
- **It connects by itself when the wait is over.** Restarting it does not shorten a wait the app asked for.
- **After a bad minute of a network, Telegram is asked again within seconds.** Where Telegram answered with an error
  of its own or could not be reached while a connection started, its client library tried twice and then waited a
  hundred seconds, then two hundred, then four hundred. Nouride now asks again itself: after a second, then two, then
  four, up to a minute apart.
- A connection nothing has answered for half a minute says that much: "It has not answered yet."

### Fixes

- **A line of steps with no answer under it is no longer sent.** While a helper worked in the background, Nouride
  woke the agent that started it to look in, the agent checked and had nothing to say, and your chat still got
  "✓ 2 steps · 42s" as a message of its own. A turn Nouride starts now writes its steps only where the agent
  speaks, in one line above its answer.
- **A helper that is at work no longer wakes the agent that started it.** That agent was woken after five
  minutes, ten, twenty and forty, by the time alone, and nearly always said nothing. It is woken now once the
  helper has gone quiet, and when the helper ends, as before.
- **"Ask Nouva to create a skill."** The button at the top of the Skills page read "to find a skill", and was not
  read as the way to have one written. It now asks your agent to write one.
- **The built-in skills are in English throughout.** Their examples of what a person might say, the sample names in
  their commands and the two document templates were partly in Indonesian. The LaTeX templates no longer load
  Indonesian hyphenation by default: for a document in another language than English the agent adds that language
  itself. What the helpers read is unchanged: dates, day names, yes and no, device and room words are still read in
  the languages they were.
