**0.8.29 updates from 0.8.0 or later.** From 0.7.x, start 0.8.0 once first:
[Upgrading to 0.8](https://nouride.com/en/docs/deployment/upgrading-to-0-8/) has the way across.

On Docker, check your configuration with the new image before you switch:
`docker run --rm -v ./nouride:/app/.nouride ghcr.io/nouverse/nouride:0.8.29 nouride config check`, with the volume your
compose file mounts.

The first start of 0.8.29 rebuilds one table of the database, the record of what people say in groups. It backs the
database up first and takes a moment; nothing in it is lost.

### A new chat page

- **One list beside the conversation.** Your orchestrator is always first, then the agents and rooms you talked to,
  the latest first, each with its cat, the last thing said and when; agents you have not talked to yet wait at the end.
  Opening an agent carries on its latest conversation. On a phone the list comes first and a conversation takes the
  whole screen.
- **Groups**, beside Chats: the groups your agents are in on Telegram, Discord and WhatsApp, one line per group however
  many of your bots sit in it, each tagged with its app in that app's colour, and the rooms you made in the dashboard.
  A chip per app narrows the list, **Dashboard** to your rooms, and groups quiet for two weeks fold into one line.
  **Pin to Chats** keeps a group you read often in Chats.
- **Anything from a chat app is read-only here**, and says so where you would type: groups and your agents' private
  chats on Telegram, Discord and WhatsApp are answered on their own app. People there show on the left under their
  names, and a group shows what people said even when no agent answered.
- **History belongs to each agent**: a drawer with only that agent's conversations, its private chats on chat apps
  among them. Each row has a menu that deletes the conversation.
- **Each agent has its own colour and its cat beside its answers**, and a tag like `@nix` is drawn in that agent's
  colour wherever it is written. On Discord a tag reads as the agent's name, not as the code Discord writes.
- **An agent's steps fold into one line** while it works: what it is doing now, how many steps and for how long. Press
  it to see them. An answer that took no steps and under a second says **instant**.
- **The side menu folds to its icons on Chats**, to give the conversation room; **Expand** opens it again and is
  remembered.
- **The dashboard installs as an app.** Over HTTPS, Chrome and Edge offer **Install**; on an iPhone, Share → Add to Home
  Screen. It opens on Chats with the mascot's head as its icon. On an iPhone you sign in once inside it.

### Changes

- **A room you made in the dashboard answers a message that tags nobody**: your orchestrator when it is in the room,
  or the room's only agent. The setting that makes groups on chat apps wait for a tag (`groups_require_mention`) no
  longer silences your own rooms; `/mention on` in a room still does. In a room your orchestrator is not in, it no
  longer answers.

### Fixes

- **OpenAI's newer models work with an OpenAI API key.** GPT-5 and later refused every reply with "Unsupported
  parameter: 'max_tokens'", and the conversation's compaction failed the same way. Nouride now sends
  `max_completion_tokens` to OpenAI's own API, and adds the setting that says so to an existing provider on it at the
  first start. Other OpenAI-compatible services (OpenRouter, Ollama, Nougate) are left as they were.
- **A group's messages are no longer lost to another group's.** Telegram numbers messages per group, and a message
  with the same number in a second group was dropped from what your agents remember of it; with two of your agents'
  bots in one group, the second one's record missed lines too.
- **A group opens at its newest message**, not its oldest, and an agent that answered in a group is listed among its
  agents.
