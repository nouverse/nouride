# Dashboard mockups: Nouride's soft look

The look chosen on 2026-11-08, before it is built: Day and Night on a laptop (`mockup.html`, `mockup.png`)
and on a phone with the tab bar (`mockup-mobile.html`, `mockup-mobile.png`). The names and tasks in them
are made up. The rules they follow are `.agents/skills/nouride-contributing/references/design-rules.md`;
the mascot's poses are in `docs/design/mascot/`.

The HTML files open in a browser on their own; they load Fredoka, Manrope and DM Mono from Google Fonts,
which the dashboard itself must not do (its fonts sit beside `main.css`).
