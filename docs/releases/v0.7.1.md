### Steering answers in the agent's own voice

A message you send while the agent works still gets "↪ Got it — I'll read that before my next step." at once. A moment later the agent adds one sentence of its own about what it will do with it, in your language and its own voice:

> ↪ Got it — I'll read that before my next step.
> On it: once this step finishes I'll do that instead and drop the rest of the plan.

- **It keeps its word.** The step that reads your message is told what the sentence promised, so "eh, skip that, do this first" changes the plan rather than being appended to it.
- **It sounds like the agent, talking to you.** The sentence is written with the agent's persona and your notes from People, so it uses the name and register you like. None of the conversation is sent: one small call beside the turn.
- **A quick turn wins.** If the turn reads your message, answers or ends before the sentence is ready, the call is stopped and nothing more is sent: what the turn did with your message is the answer. The same if the call fails or takes longer than 15 seconds. "Got it" is never held up by it.
- **On by default, and a setting** under `[queue]`:
  - `steer_reply = false` keeps "Got it" alone.
  - `steer_reply_model` names a faster model on the agent's provider for the sentence. A reasoning model can take ten seconds over one sentence; a fast one answers in about one.
  - `steer_reply_timeout_ms` (15000) is how long it may take.

### Fix

- **Turning streaming off works with every OpenAI-compatible server** ([#6](https://github.com/nouverse/nouride-releases/issues/6)). With `[llm.streaming] enabled = false`, requests now say `stream: false` outright. Some proxies, 9Router among them, streamed when the field was absent, and every turn on such a route failed with `JSON Parse error: Unexpected identifier data`. A server that streams anyway is now read as the stream it is.
