**0.8.46 updates from 0.8.0 or later.** From 0.7.x, start 0.8.0 once first:
[Upgrading to 0.8](https://nouride.com/en/docs/deployment/upgrading-to-0-8/) has the way across.

On Docker, check your configuration with the new image before you switch:
`docker run --rm -v ./nouride:/app/.nouride ghcr.io/nouverse/nouride:0.8.46 nouride config check`, with the volume your
compose file mounts.

### A mission with limits of its own

Every mission stopped to ask whether to go on at the same three limits, the ones on the Missions page. Work known to
need more had one answer: raise them for every mission.

- **An agent can draft a mission with its own limits**: hours of work, turns and tokens, each where that one mission
  stops to ask you. Ask for it ("give this one two days"), or the agent names them when the work is known to need
  more or less. Left out, a draft follows your limits as before.
- **The draft says so before you start it.** One line in its notice: "Its own limits before it asks you whether to
  go on: 24h of work (usually 12h) · 400M tokens (usually 250M)". Your Start is your yes to them. A draft with no such
  line has none of its own.
- **You change them in the draft's form**, on its page. Hours and Turns were there; Tokens is new, in millions.
- **A draft's limits keep to what the Limits form allows.** A draft took 1,000 hours, where the form refuses past 168.
- **Your limits stay as they are.** One mission's limits change nothing for the next.
- A piece of work a mission hands to another agent, and an automation's mission step, start with nobody there to say
  yes, so they keep your limits.

### Limits that fit a day of work

- **A mission asks to go on after 12 hours of work, 250 turns or 250 million tokens.** It asked after 8 hours, 100
  turns or 100 million, and long work kept stopping to ask. A limit you set yourself stays as you set it.

### Fixes

- **An agent on Google's Gemini API can use its tools.** Gemini sends a signature with a tool call and wants it back
  with the next request. Nouride did not send it back, so an agent set up on Google's own address answered a plain
  question and failed at its first tool, every time. An agent that reaches Gemini another way was not affected.
- **WhatsApp and Discord connect from behind a proxy.** Where the only way out is a proxy (`HTTPS_PROXY`), Telegram
  worked and these two never connected: they went straight out and were dropped. Both now go through the proxy, and
  `NO_PROXY` is kept to.
- **WhatsApp says it is connected when it is.** It said "connected" as soon as its own part had started, before
  WhatsApp had answered, and again after every round of tries. A WhatsApp that could not be reached looked connected
  for hours and nobody was told. It now says "connecting" with what it waits on ("Waiting for the phone to scan the
  pairing code.", or the reason the last try failed), and "connected" once WhatsApp has opened.

### Before you upgrade

- **Missions run longer before they ask**, unless you set your own limits: 12 hours, 250 turns, 250 million tokens.
  To keep the old ones, open **Missions**, then **Limits**, and set Hours 8, Turns 100 and Tokens 100.
- On Docker, run the `config check` above with the new image first.
