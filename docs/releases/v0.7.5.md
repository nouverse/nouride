### Chat platforms

- **A long answer on Telegram arrives in as few messages as it fits in.** A part that ran past Telegram's 4,096 characters once it was formatted was cut again on its own, and its overflow — often a few words — went out as a message of its own between two full ones. A 21,000-character answer that fit in six messages arrived as eleven, and with 0.7.4 sending each a second behind the last, it took eleven seconds to land instead of six. What a cut leaves now goes into the next part.

### Upgrading

- **Docker:** `docker compose pull && docker compose up -d`
- **Binary:** `nouride update --start`

No configuration or database changes.
