**0.8.45 updates from 0.8.0 or later.** From 0.7.x, start 0.8.0 once first:
[Upgrading to 0.8](https://nouride.com/en/docs/deployment/upgrading-to-0-8/) has the way across.

On Docker, check your configuration with the new image before you switch:
`docker run --rm -v ./nouride:/app/.nouride ghcr.io/nouverse/nouride:0.8.45 nouride config check`, with the volume your
compose file mounts.

A release for the automation page 0.8.44 brought, from the first day it was used to draw a real one: what you have
not saved is kept, every step says what a failure does, and the boxes beside the canvas no longer cut each other off.

### What you did not save is still there

Nothing on an automation's page is saved until you press Save, and nothing was kept either: six steps drawn, a press
on "Automations" to look at another one, and an empty page to come back to.

- **Your unsaved changes are kept in the browser**, as you make them. Open the page again and it opens on them, and
  the bar that saves says they are from before.
- **An automation you were making is on the list**, as a row of its own over the others: its name, how many steps it
  has, and **Continue** or **Discard**. A discard there has an Undo.
- **An automation with changes waiting says so on its row**: "Unsaved changes".
- **Save and Cancel end it.** Cancel on a new one, and Discard changes on one that exists, throw the kept changes away.
- **Changed by someone else while yours waited**, or by an agent's suggestion you took: the page shows the automation
  as it is saved now, and offers your older changes beside it. They are never put over it unasked.
- It is kept in that browser, for you: not on your phone, and nobody else sees it. An address another app is called
  at is not kept there.

### Every step says what a failure does

"Stop and tell me" is what a step does unless you pick another answer, and it was the one answer with nothing drawn:
pick it, and the box looked as if no answer had been given.

- **A step left at "Stop and tell me" says so in its box**, under its words, with a stop's mark: "If it fails, the
  run ends, and its chat is told why." The other answers keep their own line, each with its mark.
- **The steps that run in a step's place answer for themselves.** Each can stop, or go on anyway. Their fields used
  to say only that they ran in another step's place.
- **Where those steps end is changed where you see it, and they can go back to any step further down.** The mark
  under them, "Then back to step 3…" or "The run stops here", is pressed to pick among every later step of the line
  and a stop; the same choice is in the fields of each of those steps. It was the next step or a stop, set only in
  the fields of the step they hang on.
- **A thread is drawn from where those steps end to the step they go back to**, and the pointer on the mark lights
  that step. It was words alone.
- **A step is named by its number as it is drawn.** "Then back to “Nouva works on it”" said nothing when five steps
  shared that title, and the bar that saves counted the steps beside a step: the third box was "Step 5".

### Fixes

- **"Add a step" is never cut off.** Under a button low in the window, the list of kinds ran off the window's foot
  with its last kinds out of reach. It opens over its button when there is no room below, scrolls in itself in a
  window too short for it, stays with its button as the page scrolls, and stands under the middle of its button.
- **The box beside the canvas is one card that stays whole.** It scrolled as a whole under an edge nobody could see,
  so its top and its Done went out of sight and it looked cut in two. Its title and its Done now stay, the fields
  scroll between them, and it is as tall as the window has room for instead of stopping a hand's width short.
- **An agent is picked by face, and by typing.** Who does a step, and who runs the automation, were lists of names.
  Each agent has its face now, and a field narrows the list as you type.
- **The bar that saves takes you to what is missing.** "Give it a name." is pressed, and the name's field is in front
  of you; a step that lacks something opens.
- **A list opened inside a card closes when its button scrolls out of the card**, as one whose button left the
  window does. It used to follow the button and lie over the card's title.
- **An automation written at the command line or by an agent, whose side steps go on after a failure, is drawn as
  side steps.** One such step used to put all of them on the main line, with jumps between them.

### Before you upgrade

- Nothing to do. On Docker, run the `config check` above with the new image first.
