**0.8.20 updates from 0.8.0 or later.** From 0.7.x, start 0.8.0 once first:
[Upgrading to 0.8](https://nouride.com/en/docs/deployment/upgrading-to-0-8/) has the way across.

On Docker, check your configuration with the new image before you switch:
`docker run --rm -v ./nouride:/app/.nouride ghcr.io/nouverse/nouride:0.8.20 nouride config check`, with the volume your
compose file mounts.

### Knowledge base

- **Documents your agents look things up in.** Keep manuals, SOPs, price lists, policies or lab results in collections,
  and your agents search them when a question is about what they cover, saying which document and which part an answer
  came from. Markdown, text, CSV, web pages, PDF, Word, Excel and PowerPoint, found by their words and, when search by
  meaning is set up, by what they mean. On Docker, PDF and Office files need Python, which the page offers to install.
- **Made where you are.** On the dashboard, under the new **Knowledge & Memory**: make a collection, drop files on it or
  write a document. In a chat: send a file or a link and ask the agent to keep it. With `nouride knowledge` on the
  command line, or by putting files in a collection's folder.
- **Closed until you open it.** A collection is answered from only for the person who made it, the people in the chat it
  was made from, or the admins for a folder made by hand, until an admin opens it to everyone or a group. One kept for a
  person is never used in a group. An agent asked to keep something in a wider collection waits for an admin's yes.
- **A document about someone** is listed with what is known about them under Knowledge & Memory, and found first when
  the conversation is about them.
- **Related documents and the graph:** an open document shows the ones closest in meaning, and the memory graph can
  show your collections and documents beside your conversations.
- Collections can be archived, and are backed up with your memory.

### Chats

- **`/btw` answers as your agent.** A side question is answered in the agent's own voice and your language, from the
  conversation and the work in progress, quickly, and does nothing: ask it to do something and it tells you to say so in
  the chat. Before, a side question on an agent that had just started a conversation could time out and answer in
  English that it needed more.
- **The quick reply to a message sent while the agent works reads the conversation.** It no longer misreads "that one"
  or a question about something said a few messages earlier.

### Dashboard

- **When the server refuses something, the page says why in everyday words**, and what you can do; the server's own
  message is under Details.
- **Missions are cards**, laid out as the Knowledge tab's collections: how each stands, its steps, what it is doing or
  waiting for, and Open.
- **The first agent is a Pilot named NouridePilot**, its headline and personality filled in with the Pilot's, in the
  setup on the dashboard and with `nouride setup`.
